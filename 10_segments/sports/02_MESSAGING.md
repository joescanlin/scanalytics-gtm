## Value Proposition
PerformanceFloor gives sports science teams continuous biomechanical data on every athlete, every session. Unlike periodic testing, our floor-based sensors capture movement patterns in real training environments, enabling proactive injury prevention and performance optimization.

## Key Benefits (Proposed)

### 1. Continuous Movement Capture
**Feature**: Every step, jump, and movement recorded
**Outcome**: Complete picture of athlete load and movement quality across all sessions

### 2. Injury Risk Detection
**Feature**: AI analysis of movement asymmetries and pattern changes
**Outcome**: Identify injury risks before they become injuries

### 3. Return-to-Play Tracking
**Feature**: Objective movement comparison to pre-injury baselines
**Outcome**: Data-driven return-to-play decisions

### 4. Performance Optimization
**Feature**: Detailed force and timing analytics
**Outcome**: Optimize training for sport-specific movement patterns
