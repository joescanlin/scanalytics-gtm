## Discovery Questions

### Situation
1. What sports science staff do you have?
2. What movement/gait analysis do you currently use?
3. What performance platforms do you use for data?
4. Do you have a dedicated training facility?

### Problem
5. How do you currently monitor athlete load beyond macro metrics?
6. What's your biggest challenge with injury prevention?
7. How do you make return-to-play decisions?
8. What gaps exist in your current performance data?

### Implication
9. What does a preventable soft tissue injury cost (player value, season impact)?
10. How do incomplete RTH decisions affect injury recurrence?
11. What competitive disadvantage comes from data gaps?

### Need-Payoff
12. If you could see movement pattern changes before injuries occur, how would that change your program?
13. What would objective RTH data be worth for decision confidence?
14. How would continuous movement data change your training optimization?

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Force Plates
"Force plates give you snapshots during testing. PerformanceFloor gives you the movie - continuous data during actual training."

### vs. Motion Capture Labs
"You can't put a Vicon system in your weight room. PerformanceFloor captures training movements in real training environments."

### vs. Wearables/GPS
"Wearables tell you how far and fast. We tell you how - movement quality, ground contact, asymmetries."

### vs. Nothing
"You're making decisions about million-dollar athletes with incomplete data. Soft tissue injuries are often preceded by movement changes you can't see but we can measure."
