# Sports Performance - Objections & Responses

## Objection 1: "We already use force plates and motion capture."

### Response
Force plates give you great data for single-point testing. PerformanceFloor gives you continuous data during actual training. You're not comparing apples to apples - we complement your existing testing with always-on monitoring between tests.

---

## Objection 2: "Our athletes already wear GPS/wearables."

### Response
GPS and wearables give you macro load data - distance, speed, heart rate. PerformanceFloor gives you micro movement data - how they're actually moving, ground contact patterns, asymmetries. It's a different layer of insight that wearables can't capture.

---

## Objection 3: "How does it work in a training facility?"

### Response
PerformanceFloor installs beneath your existing flooring in key training areas - weight room, courts, turf. Athletes train normally while the floor captures every step. No behavior change required, no devices to wear or charge.

---

## Objection 4: "Can it integrate with our performance platform?"

### Response
Yes, we have APIs for integration with major sports performance platforms. Movement data can flow into your existing analytics ecosystem alongside GPS, wellness, and other metrics.

---

## Objection 5: "How accurate is it compared to a gait lab?"

### Response
Our technology is validated against gold-standard motion capture systems with millimeter-level accuracy. The difference is we capture this data continuously during training, not just during periodic lab visits.

---

## Objection 6: "This seems like overkill."

### Response
We work with you to determine the right coverage. Key training areas - weight room platforms, specific court zones - can give you most of the value without instrumenting every square foot.

---
