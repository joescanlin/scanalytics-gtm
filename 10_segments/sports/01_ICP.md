# Sports Performance - Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer
**Director of Performance / Head of Sports Science**

### Firmographics
- **Organization type**: Professional sports teams, elite college programs, national federations
- **Facility**: Training facility, performance center
- **Budget**: Performance technology budget available
- **Sports**: All sports with gait/movement relevance (running, basketball, football, soccer, etc.)

### Psychographics
- Data-obsessed, always seeking edge
- Early adopter of performance technology
- Focused on injury prevention and return-to-play
- Values objective performance metrics
- Accountable for athlete outcomes

### Decision Criteria
1. Performance insight quality
2. Injury prevention capability
3. Integration with existing analytics
4. Athlete/coach adoption
5. Research validation

---

## Secondary Buyers

### Head Coach
- Ultimate authority on team decisions
- Needs buy-in for technology adoption
- Focused on competitive advantage

### Athletic Trainer / Team Physician
- Injury prevention focus
- Return-to-play decisions
- Medical data integration

### General Manager / Director of Athletics
- Budget authority
- Organization-wide technology decisions
- ROI focus

---

## Qualification Criteria

### Must-Haves
- [ ] Dedicated performance/sports science staff
- [ ] Budget for performance technology
- [ ] Interest in gait/movement analytics
- [ ] Decision timeline within 6 months

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Current injury prevention challenges
- [ ] Multiple sports/teams (portfolio opportunity)
- [ ] Research partnership interest
- [ ] New facility construction planned
