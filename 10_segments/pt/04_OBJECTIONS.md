## Objection 8: "What about patient privacy?"

### Response
SmartStep uses no cameras, microphones, or wearable devices. The floor-based sensors detect movement patterns anonymously - we never capture images or identify individuals. Data is stored in HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, and you control access permissions.

### Proof Points
- No cameras or wearables
- HIPAA-compliant
- Anonymous movement data
