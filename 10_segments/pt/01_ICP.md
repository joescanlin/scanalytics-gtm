# Physical Therapy - Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer
**PT Clinic Owner / Practice Manager**

### Firmographics
- **Clinic size**: 5,000-25,000 sq ft
- **Patient volume**: 50+ patients/week
- **Staff**: 3+ treating therapists
- **Revenue**: $500K+ annually
- **Setting**: Outpatient, private practice, or hospital-affiliated outpatient

### Psychographics
- Data-driven approach to patient care
- Frustrated with documentation burden
- Looking for competitive differentiation
- Values evidence-based practice
- Concerned about reimbursement pressures
- Interested in outcome tracking for payer negotiations

### Decision Criteria
1. Clinical utility (will therapists use it?)
2. ROI and payback period
3. Integration with existing EMR/EHR
4. Implementation disruption
5. Staff training requirements

---

## Secondary Buyers

### Director of Rehab (Hospital System)
- Manages multiple PT/OT departments
- Budget authority for clinical equipment
- Focused on outcomes reporting and quality metrics
- Answers to C-suite on efficiency and outcomes

### Physical Therapist (Influencer)
- End user of the technology
- Cares about clinical utility and time savings
- Values objective outcome measures
- May champion or block purchase decision

---

## Qualification Criteria

### Must-Haves
- [ ] Active interest in gait analysis or objective outcomes
- [ ] Budget authority or influence
- [ ] Timeline within 6 months
- [ ] Floor renovation planned or willingness to retrofit

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Currently using manual gait assessment
- [ ] Documentation time complaints
- [ ] Reimbursement challenges they're trying to solve
- [ ] Multiple locations (expansion opportunity)

---

## Anti-Patterns (Disqualifiers)
- Solo practitioner (too small for ROI)
- Primarily home health or mobile PT
- Very recent floor installation (unlikely to retrofit)
- No interest in objective measures ("we trust clinical judgment")
- Extremely price-sensitive / no budget for technology

---

## Entry Points

### Events
- APTA (American Physical Therapy Association) conferences
- Private Practice Section events
- State PT association meetings
- Rehab technology conferences

### Publications/Channels
- PT in Motion magazine
- APTA publications
- LinkedIn PT communities
- Physical therapy blogs and podcasts

### Partners
- EMR/EHR vendors (integration partnerships)
- Flooring contractors specializing in healthcare
- PT consulting firms
- Practice management consultants

---

## Buying Process (Typical)

1. **Awareness**: See demo at conference, referral from colleague, web search
2. **Interest**: Request demo, evaluate clinical use cases
3. **Consideration**: Trial period or pilot, staff input, ROI analysis
4. **Decision**: Budget approval, contract negotiation
5. **Implementation**: Installation scheduling, training, go-live
6. **Expansion**: Add locations, referrals to peers

**Typical timeline**: 3-6 months from first contact to installation
