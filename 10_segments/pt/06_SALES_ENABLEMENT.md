# Physical Therapy - Sales Enablement

**Product**: SmartStep for Physical Therapy

**Primary Buyer**: PT Clinic Owners, Practice Managers

**Sales Cycle**: 3-6 months

## Discovery Questions

### Situation
1. How many therapists do you have on staff?
2. What's your average patient volume per week?
3. What EMR/EHR system are you using?
4. When was your clinic floor last updated?

### Problem
5. How much time do your therapists spend on documentation per patient?
6. How do you currently assess gait and movement?
7. What challenges do you face with outcome tracking?
8. How do you demonstrate progress to payers for reimbursement?

### Implication
9. What does documentation burden cost you in therapist time?
10. How does lack of objective data affect payer negotiations?
11. Have you lost patients to competitors with more technology?

### Need-Payoff
12. If you could save 5 hours per therapist per week on documentation, what would that mean for your practice?
13. What would objective outcome data be worth for your payer relationships?

- Propose pilot or next steps

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Manual Gait Assessment
"SmartStep captures objective data continuously, not just during scheduled assessments. You get trends over time, not snapshots."

### vs. Motion Capture Labs
"No setup time, no calibration, no dedicated space. SmartStep captures gait data during regular therapy sessions."

### vs. Wearable Gait Sensors
"Patients don't need to remember to wear anything. The floor is always measuring."

### vs. Instrumented Walkways
"Full clinic coverage, not just a 20-foot mat. See gait patterns in real-world movement, not just controlled walks."

## Pricing Discussion

### Framing
"Most clinics invest $150-350/month for SmartStep - less than one therapist hour per week in documentation savings."

### ROI Calculator Inputs
- Current documentation time per patient
- Therapist hourly rate
- Patients per week
- Number of therapists

### Sample ROI
- 5 therapists x 5 hours/week saved = 25 hours/week
- 25 hours x $40/hour = $1,000/week saved
- $4,000/month in time savings vs. $300/month SmartStep cost
- 13x ROI on time savings alone
