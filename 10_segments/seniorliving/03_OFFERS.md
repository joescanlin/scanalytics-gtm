## Lead Magnets (Proposed)

### 1. Fall Prevention ROI Calculator
**Type**: Interactive tool
**Hook**: "Calculate the true cost of falls in your community"
**Inputs**: Resident count, current fall rate, average fall cost
**Outputs**: Potential savings, ROI projection

### 2. Guide: Technology Approaches to Fall Prevention
**Type**: PDF download
**Hook**: "Compare cameras, wearables, and smart flooring for senior living"
**Contents**:
- Pros/cons of each approach
- Privacy and dignity considerations
- Implementation factors
- Case studies

### 3. Webinar: Addressing the Staffing Crisis with Technology
**Type**: Live/recorded webinar
**Hook**: "How leading communities are doing more with less"
**Contents**:
- Staffing challenge statistics
- Technology augmentation strategies
- SmartStep demo for staff efficiency
- Q&A

## Pilot Program

### Structure
- Single community pilot
- 30% sensor coverage
- 90-day evaluation
- Full dashboard access
- Weekly check-ins
- Success metrics defined upfront

### Pilot Success Criteria
- Fall detection accuracy
- Staff adoption rate
- Response time improvement
- Operational insights value

### Pilot to Full Deployment
- Expansion within pilot community
- Additional community rollout
- Portfolio agreement
