# Senior Living - Sales Enablement

## Segment Overview
**Product**: SmartStep for Senior Living
**URL**: seniorliving.scanalyticsinc.com
**Primary Buyer**: Executive Directors, Operators

**Sales Cycle**: 6-12 months (pilot common)

---

## Discovery Questions

### Situation
1. How many units/beds in your community?
2. What care levels do you offer (IL, AL, MC)?
3. What fall detection/monitoring do you currently use?
4. What nurse call system do you have?

### Problem
5. What's your current fall rate? Fall with injury rate?
6. How do you handle overnight monitoring?
7. What challenges do you face with pendant compliance?
8. How is staffing affecting your care delivery?

### Implication
9. What does a fall with injury cost your community (liability, staffing, reputation)?
10. How do fall incidents affect family trust and referrals?
11. What's the impact of staffing challenges on resident care?

### Need-Payoff
12. If you could detect every fall within seconds, how would that change your outcomes?
13. What would 20% staff efficiency improvement mean for your community?
14. How would comprehensive monitoring help your marketing and family relations?

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Pendant/PERS Systems
"SmartStep provides 100% coverage regardless of compliance. When Mrs. Smith doesn't wear her pendant to the bathroom at 3am, we still detect if she falls."

### vs. Camera Systems
"Residents and families don't want cameras in private spaces. SmartStep provides comprehensive monitoring while preserving dignity."

### vs. Motion Sensors
"Motion sensors tell you someone is in the room. SmartStep tells you if they fell, how their gait is changing, and when something's wrong."

### vs. Bed/Chair Sensors
"Bed sensors only work in bed. SmartStep covers the entire journey - bathroom, hallway, common areas."

## ROI Framework

### Fall Cost Reduction
- Average fall cost: $35,000-$50,000 (with injury and liability)
- Communities with 100 units: 50-100 falls/year
- Even 10% reduction = $175,000-$500,000 savings
- SmartStep annual cost for 100-unit community: ~$20,000

### Staff Efficiency
- Reduced response time = better outcomes
- Optimized cleaning schedules = 15-20% labor savings
- Better monitoring = fewer night shift needs

### Energy Savings
- Occupancy-based HVAC optimization
- Claimed 35% energy reduction (needs validation)

### Marketing/Census Value
- Technology differentiation
- Family peace of mind
- Higher star ratings

## Pilot Program Details

### Standard Pilot
- 90-day evaluation
- 30% of community coverage
- Single memory care or high-risk unit
- Full dashboard access
- Weekly check-ins

### Pilot Metrics
- Falls detected vs. total falls
- Response time improvement
- Staff feedback scores
- False positive rate
- Family satisfaction

### Pilot Success Criteria
- 90%+ fall detection rate
- <2 min average response time improvement
- 80%+ staff satisfaction
- Clear path to full deployment ROI
