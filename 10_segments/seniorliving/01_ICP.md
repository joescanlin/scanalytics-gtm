# Senior Living - Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer
**Senior Living Operator / Executive Director**

### Firmographics
- **Community size**: 50-200+ units/beds
- **Facility type**: Independent Living, Assisted Living, Memory Care, CCRC
- **Square footage**: 25,000-150,000+ sq ft
- **Staff**: 20+ caregiving staff
- **Ownership**: Private operators, regional chains, national portfolios

### Psychographics
- Focused on resident safety and quality of care
- Balancing staffing challenges with care quality
- Concerned about liability and fall incidents
- Looking for technology to augment limited staff
- Values family satisfaction and retention
- Data-driven for quality metrics and regulatory compliance

### Decision Criteria
1. Resident safety improvement (fall reduction)
2. Staff efficiency and workload
3. Implementation disruption to residents
4. Integration with existing nurse call systems
5. ROI and budget fit
6. Regulatory compliance support

---

## Secondary Buyers

### Director of Nursing / Care
- Clinical leader for care delivery
- Primary user of monitoring data
- Focused on care quality and outcomes
- Answers to ED on clinical metrics

### VP of Operations (Portfolio)
- Multi-community decision maker
- Standardization across properties
- Budget authority for larger deals
- Focused on scalability and ROI

### Regional Director
- Manages multiple communities
- Pilot authority
- Expansion champion

---

## Qualification Criteria

### Must-Haves
- [ ] Active concern about fall incidents
- [ ] Budget authority or influence
- [ ] Interest in resident monitoring technology
- [ ] Decision timeline within 6-12 months

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Recent fall incidents with liability concern
- [ ] Staffing challenges
- [ ] Renovation or new construction planned
- [ ] Multiple communities (portfolio opportunity)
- [ ] Current dissatisfaction with pendant/wearable systems

---

## Anti-Patterns (Disqualifiers)
- Very small community (<30 units) - ROI challenging
- No technology budget
- Recent major technology investment in competing solution
- Strong preference for camera-based systems
- No concern about fall rates

## Buying Process (Typical)

1. **Awareness**: Conference, referral, incident-driven research
2. **Interest**: Initial demo, stakeholder introduction
3. **Evaluation**: Pilot in one community, staff feedback
4. **Decision**: Corporate approval, contract negotiation
5. **Implementation**: Phased rollout, training
6. **Expansion**: Additional communities, referrals

**Typical timeline**: 6-12 months from first contact to contract
**Pilot**: Common requirement, 3-6 month evaluation period
