# Senior Living - Objections & Responses

## Objection 1: "We already have pendant/wearable systems."

### Response
Pendants work when residents wear them - studies show compliance rates below 50%. SmartStep works 24/7 because it's built into the floor. We complement your existing system by catching falls when pendants aren't worn or aren't pressed.

### Proof Points
- 100% coverage when floor is installed
- No compliance or battery issues
- Works alongside existing systems

### Follow-up Question
"What's your current pendant compliance rate, and have you had incidents where pendants weren't worn or pressed?"

---

## Objection 2: "How is SmartStep installed?"

### Response
SmartStep can be installed by any flooring contractor - it's a simple installation that doesn't require advanced technical skills. The sensor layer goes beneath your existing flooring, and installation is typically completed with minimal disruption to residents and operations.

### Proof Points
- Standard flooring contractor installation
- Works with existing flooring
- Minimal disruption

### Follow-up Question
"Do you have any renovations or flooring projects planned that we could coordinate with?"

---

## Objection 3: "Does it require cameras or wearables?"

### Response
No - SmartStep uses sensors printed into the underlayment beneath your flooring. There are no cameras, microphones, or wearable devices. This preserves resident privacy and dignity while providing comprehensive monitoring.

### Proof Points
- No cameras or microphones
- No wearables required
- Privacy-preserving by design

### Follow-up Question
"How have your residents responded to other monitoring technologies you've tried?"

## Objection 4: "How does it detect falls?"

### Response
Our proprietary technology uses advanced algorithms to analyze contact patterns on the floor. It can distinguish between normal activities - sitting, lying down intentionally - and actual fall events. When a fall is detected, designated staff are immediately alerted with the precise location.

### Proof Points
- Advanced pattern recognition
- Distinguishes falls from normal activities
- Instant alerts with location

### Follow-up Question
"How do you currently handle fall detection, and what's your average response time?"

### Proof Points
