# Healthcare - Objections & Responses

## Objection 1: "Is it HIPAA compliant?"

### Response
Yes, SmartStep is designed with HIPAA compliance in mind. We collect no personally identifiable information - the floor sensors detect movement patterns, not identities. All data is stored in HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with enterprise-grade encryption and access controls.

---

## Objection 2: "How is it installed in a busy hospital?"

### Response
SmartStep can be installed by any flooring contractor with minimal disruption. We typically work during off-hours and can phase installation by unit. The sensor layer goes beneath existing flooring, so no visible changes to patient rooms.

---

## Objection 3: "Does it integrate with our EMR?"

### Response
Yes, SmartStep can integrate with major EMR systems including Epic and Cerner. Fall events and mobility data can flow directly into patient records, supporting documentation and quality reporting.

---

## Objection 4: "We have bed alarms and nurse call buttons."

### Response
Bed alarms only work in bed, and call buttons only work if pressed. SmartStep monitors the entire patient journey - from bed to bathroom to hallway. We catch falls when patients don't press buttons and when they're not in bed.

---

## Objection 5: "What about power outages?"

### Response
SmartStep includes battery backup for power outages and cellular backup for network failures. In a healthcare environment where continuous monitoring is critical, we maintain operation even when infrastructure fails.

---

## Objection 6: "Our nurses are too busy for another system."

### Response
SmartStep integrates with your existing nurse call system - alerts appear where nurses already look. There's no separate system to monitor. We add intelligence to existing workflows, not complexity.
