## Discovery Questions

### Situation
1. How many beds in your facility/system?
2. What fall prevention programs do you have?
3. What's your current fall rate?
4. What EMR system do you use?

### Problem
5. What are your biggest patient safety challenges?
6. How do fall incidents affect your quality scores?
7. What's your current fall-related liability exposure?
8. How effective are bed alarms and nurse call buttons?

### Implication
9. What does a fall with injury cost (direct + indirect)?
10. How do fall rates affect CMS reimbursement?
11. What's the staff time spent on fall prevention protocols?

### Need-Payoff
12. If you could detect 95% of falls within seconds, how would outcomes change?
13. What would automated documentation be worth for compliance?
14. How would comprehensive mobility data support care decisions?

## ROI Framework

### Fall Cost Reduction
- Average hospital fall with injury: $35,000-$50,000
- 200-bed hospital: ~200-400 falls/year
- 20% reduction = $1.4M-$4M savings
- SmartStep annual cost: $100K-$200K

### Quality Score Impact
- Fall rates affect CMS reimbursement
- HAC Reduction Program penalties
- Star ratings

### Liability Reduction
- Litigation costs (settlements, legal fees)
- Insurance premiums
