# Healthcare - Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer
**Hospital Administrator / CNO (Chief Nursing Officer)**

### Firmographics
- **Facility size**: 100+ beds
- **Type**: Acute care hospitals, rehabilitation facilities, long-term acute care
- **Square footage**: 100,000-1,000,000+ sq ft
- **Setting**: Single hospital or health system

### Psychographics
- Patient safety is paramount
- Regulatory compliance driven
- Concerned about fall-related liability
- Looking for technology to improve outcomes
- Data-driven quality improvement focus
- Budget pressure but willing to invest in safety

### Decision Criteria
1. Patient safety improvement (fall reduction)
2. Regulatory compliance support
3. HIPAA compliance
4. Integration with existing EMR and systems
5. ROI and liability reduction
6. Staff workflow impact

---

## Secondary Buyers

### VP of Quality / Patient Safety Officer

### CFO / Finance

## Qualification Criteria

### Must-Haves
- [ ] Active concern about patient falls
- [ ] Budget authority or influence
- [ ] HIPAA compliance capable (we are)
- [ ] Decision timeline within 12 months

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Recent fall incidents or litigation
- [ ] Quality improvement initiative underway
- [ ] Construction or renovation planned
- [ ] Multiple facilities in system
