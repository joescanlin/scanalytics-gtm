## Lead Magnets (Proposed)

### 1. Hospital Fall Prevention Technology Guide
**Type**: PDF whitepaper
**Hook**: "Compare fall prevention technologies for acute care settings"
**Contents**: Technology comparison, privacy considerations, implementation factors

### 2. Fall Cost Calculator
**Type**: Interactive tool
**Hook**: "Calculate the true cost of patient falls in your facility"
**Inputs**: Bed count, fall rate, average fall cost
**Outputs**: Annual cost, potential savings with reduction

### 3. HIPAA Compliance Checklist
**Type**: PDF checklist
**Hook**: "Ensure your fall detection technology meets HIPAA requirements"
