## Discovery Questions

### Situation
1. How large is your facility/portfolio (sq ft)?
2. What occupancy tracking do you currently have?
3. What BMS/IWMS systems do you use?
4. How do you currently schedule cleaning and maintenance?

### Problem
5. How accurate is your current occupancy data?
6. What's your biggest facilities cost challenge?
7. How do you handle return-to-office space planning?
8. What are your sustainability/energy goals?

### Implication
9. What does over-cleaning or over-conditioning empty spaces cost?
10. How does inaccurate space data affect real estate decisions?
11. What happens when you can't demonstrate ESG progress?

### Need-Payoff
12. If you could clean based on actual traffic, what would you save?
13. What would accurate space utilization data be worth for portfolio decisions?
14. How would occupancy-based HVAC affect your energy costs?

## ROI Framework

### Cleaning Optimization
- Typical building cleans 100% daily
- With traffic data: clean high-traffic 100%, low-traffic 50%
- Potential 15-25% cleaning labor reduction
- 50,000 sq ft building, $2/sq ft cleaning = $100K/year
- 20% savings = $20K/year

### Energy Savings
- HVAC based on schedule vs. occupancy
- Claimed 35% energy reduction
- Need case study validation

### Space Optimization
- Identify underutilized space
- Avoid unnecessary expansion
- Real estate cost avoidance
