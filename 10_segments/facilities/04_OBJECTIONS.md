# Facilities Management - Objections & Responses

## Objection 1: "How accurate is the occupancy tracking?"

### Response
SmartStep provides enterprise-grade occupancy tracking with high precision. We capture detailed foot traffic patterns, space utilization, and peak usage times. Unlike badge systems that only track card holders or ceiling sensors with limited accuracy, our floor-based approach captures 100% of foot traffic.

---

## Objection 2: "Can it integrate with our BMS?"

### Response
Yes, SmartStep is designed to integrate with major Building Management Systems. Our API allows seamless transfer of occupancy data to enable automated HVAC optimization, lighting control, and other building systems.

---

## Objection 3: "We already have occupancy sensors."

### Response
Traditional ceiling sensors give you room-level presence data. SmartStep gives you actual traffic patterns, flow analysis, and zone-level precision. You can see not just that a space was occupied, but how it was used and by how many people.

---

## Objection 4: "Do we need to cover the whole building?"

### Response
No - that's one of our key advantages. Less than 10% building coverage in strategic locations (entrances, high-traffic areas, key zones) gives you comprehensive insights. We work with you to determine optimal placement.

---

## Objection 5: "What about privacy concerns?"

### Response
SmartStep uses no cameras and collects no personally identifiable information. The floor sensors detect foot traffic patterns anonymously - we never identify individuals. This makes us ideal for environments where camera surveillance is inappropriate.

---

## Objection 6: "How does predictive maintenance work?"

### Response
Our AI analyzes foot traffic patterns to predict wear and tear on different areas. High-traffic zones need more frequent cleaning and maintenance. We help you allocate resources based on actual usage, not fixed schedules.

---

## Objection 7: "This seems like overkill for our needs."

### Response
We scale to your needs. Start with a pilot in one zone to prove value, then expand based on results. Most facilities see clear ROI from cleaning optimization alone, with additional value from energy savings and maintenance planning.
