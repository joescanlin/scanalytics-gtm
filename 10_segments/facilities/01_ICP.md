# Facilities Management - Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Buyer
**Facility Manager / Director of Facilities**

### Firmographics
- **Building size**: 50,000-500,000+ sq ft
- **Building type**: Corporate offices, commercial, education, hospitality
- **Staff**: Dedicated facilities/operations team
- **Ownership**: Owner-occupied, property management, corporate RE

### Psychographics
- Data-driven approach to operations
- Focused on cost optimization
- Balancing tenant/employee experience with efficiency
- Interested in sustainability and energy reduction
- Responsive to executive pressure on real estate costs

### Decision Criteria
1. ROI and cost savings (cleaning, energy, maintenance)
2. Integration with existing BMS/IWMS
3. Implementation disruption
4. Data quality and accuracy
5. Scalability across portfolio

---

## Secondary Buyers

### VP of Real Estate / Corporate RE
- Portfolio-level decisions
- Budget authority
- Focused on space utilization and cost per square foot

### Director of Operations
- Day-to-day operational efficiency
- Staff management
- Service quality

### Sustainability / ESG Lead
- Energy reduction targets
- Sustainability reporting
- Carbon footprint reduction

---

## Qualification Criteria

### Must-Haves
- [ ] Active interest in space optimization or occupancy data
- [ ] Budget for facilities technology
- [ ] Decision timeline within 6 months
- [ ] Willing to pilot

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Return-to-office planning challenges
- [ ] Energy cost concerns
- [ ] Cleaning efficiency goals
- [ ] Multiple buildings (portfolio)
