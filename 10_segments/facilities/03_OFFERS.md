## Lead Magnets (Proposed)

### 1. Space Utilization Benchmark Report
**Type**: PDF report
**Hook**: "How does your space utilization compare to industry benchmarks?"
**Contents**: Industry data, optimization strategies, case studies

### 2. Cleaning Efficiency Calculator
**Type**: Interactive tool
**Hook**: "Calculate potential cleaning labor savings with traffic-based scheduling"
**Inputs**: Square footage, current cleaning frequency, labor costs
**Outputs**: Potential savings estimate

### 3. Energy Savings Assessment
**Type**: Consultation offer
**Hook**: "Free assessment of energy savings potential with occupancy-based HVAC"
**Deliverable**: Custom analysis for their building
