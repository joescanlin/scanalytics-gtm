### What We Do

**Platform Level**
> Our DOE-backed, patented Smart Flooring system captures occupancy and movement to optimize your building. Sensors embedded in our own patented underlayment work beneath your flooring, connecting to WiFi or network infrastructure to deliver real-time insights through an intuitive dashboard.

### How It Works

**4-Step Process**
1. **Install**: Thin sensor layer installs beneath any flooring type
2. **Connect**: System connects to your WiFi or network
3. **Learn**: AI learns normal patterns within two weeks
4. **Insight**: Real-time dashboard delivers actionable analytics

**Installation Detail**
> SmartStep can be installed by any flooring contractor. It is a simple installation that does not require advanced technical skills. The installation is quick and non-disruptive, typically completed within a day.

## FAQs by Segment

### Universal FAQs

**How is SmartStep installed?**
> SmartStep can be installed by any flooring contractor. It is a simple installation that does not require advanced technical skills. The sensor layer goes beneath your existing flooring, and installation is quick and non-disruptive.

**Does SmartStep require cameras or wearable devices?**
> No. SmartStep uses sensors printed into the underlayment beneath your flooring to detect movement patterns. There are no cameras, microphones, or wearable devices required.

**Will SmartStep work during a power outage?**
> Yes. The SmartStep system includes battery backup that keeps the system operational during power outages as well as cellular backup for network failures.

### PT-Specific FAQs

**How accurate is the gait analysis?**
> SmartStep provides clinical-grade gait analysis with millimeter-level accuracy. Our system captures detailed spatiotemporal parameters including step length, width, velocity, and symmetry, validated against gold-standard motion capture systems.

**How does the SOAP note automation work?**
> Our AI system automatically captures and analyzes all movement data during a session, including exercise performance, gait parameters, and progress metrics. This data is intelligently compiled into comprehensive SOAP notes that can be reviewed and edited by the therapist before being finalized.

### Senior Living-Specific FAQs

**How does SmartStep detect falls?**
> Our proprietary technology uses advanced algorithms to analyze contact patterns on the floor. It can distinguish between normal activities and fall events, automatically alerting designated contacts when a potential fall is detected.

**Does it integrate with nurse call systems?**
> Yes, SmartStep can be integrated with existing nurse call systems to provide real-time alerts to caregivers when a fall is detected or other events occur.

### Healthcare-Specific FAQs

**Is SmartStep HIPAA compliant?**
> Yes. SmartStep collects no personally identifiable information - the floor sensors detect movement patterns, not identities. All data is stored in HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with enterprise-grade encryption.

**Does it integrate with EMR systems?**
> Yes. SmartStep can integrate with major EMR systems including Epic and Cerner. Fall events and mobility data can flow directly into patient records.

### Facilities-Specific FAQs

**How much of the building needs sensor coverage?**
> Less than 10% building coverage is typically required. Strategic placement in high-traffic areas, entrances, and key zones provides comprehensive insights without instrumenting the entire building.

**Can it integrate with our Building Management System?**
> Yes. SmartStep is designed to integrate with major Building Management Systems via API, enabling automated HVAC optimization, lighting control, and other building systems.
