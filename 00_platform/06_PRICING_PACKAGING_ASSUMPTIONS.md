# Pricing & Packaging Assumptions

## Current Pricing Model (from Senior Living site)

### Annual Subscription
| Building Size | 1-Year Term | 2-Year Term | 3+ Year Term |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Base rate | $0.40/sq ft | $0.35/sq ft | $0.30/sq ft |
| 25,000+ sq ft | -$0.03 | -$0.03 | -$0.03 |
| 50,000+ sq ft | -$0.05 | -$0.05 | -$0.05 |
| Multi-building (2) | -$0.02 | -$0.02 | -$0.02 |
| Multi-building (3+) | -$0.05 | -$0.05 | -$0.05 |
| **Minimum** | $0.25/sq ft | $0.25/sq ft | $0.25/sq ft |

### One-Time Installation
- **Rate**: $0.40 per square foot
- **Coverage**: Typically 15-30% of total building area (strategic placement)
- **Includes**: Adhesive, labor, sensor placement, configuration, calibration, testing

### What's Included in Subscription
- Complete hardware (sensors already installed)
- Software platform access for all staff
- Mobile app for on-the-go monitoring
- Regular system updates and maintenance
- Technical support and training
- Analytics and reporting dashboard

## Pricing Examples

### Small PT Clinic (5,000 sq ft)
- **Installation**: 5,000 x 30% x $0.40 = $600 one-time
- **Annual subscription**: 5,000 x $0.40 = $2,000/year
- **Monthly equivalent**: ~$167/month
- **First year total**: $2,600

### Mid-size Senior Living (50,000 sq ft)
- **Installation**: 50,000 x 30% x $0.40 = $6,000 one-time
- **Annual subscription (1-yr)**: 50,000 x $0.35 = $17,500/year
- **Monthly equivalent**: ~$1,458/month
- **First year total**: $23,500

### Large Hospital System (3 buildings, 150,000 sq ft total)
- **Installation**: 150,000 x 30% x $0.40 = $18,000 one-time
- **Annual subscription (3-yr, multi-building)**: 150,000 x $0.25 = $37,500/year
- **Monthly equivalent**: ~$3,125/month
- **First year total**: $55,500
