# Competitive Landscape

## Overview
Scanalytics competes across multiple categories depending on the segment. There is no single direct competitor with floor-based sensor technology at scale.

---

## Competitive Categories

### 1. Fall Detection / Senior Monitoring
**Competitors** (categories, not endorsements of specific companies):
- Wearable pendants and watches (PERS - Personal Emergency Response Systems)
- Camera-based fall detection systems
- Motion sensor networks
- Bed/chair sensors

**Our Positioning**:
> "Unlike wearables that require compliance or cameras that invade privacy, SmartStep is always on and completely invisible."

**Competitive Advantages**:
| Factor | Wearables | Cameras | Motion Sensors | SmartStep |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------------|-----------|
| 24/7 coverage | Only if worn | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Privacy | Medium | Low | High | High |
| Resident acceptance | Low-Medium | Low | High | High |
| Gait/movement data | No | Limited | No | Yes |
| Maintenance | High (batteries, lost) | Medium | Low | Low |

---

### 2. Occupancy & Space Management
**Competitors** (categories):
- Occupancy sensors (ceiling-mounted)
- Badge/access card systems
- WiFi/Bluetooth tracking
- Camera-based people counting
- Desk booking platforms with sensors

**Our Positioning**:
> "Get accurate occupancy data without the privacy concerns of cameras or the coverage gaps of badge systems."

**Competitive Advantages**:
| Factor | Ceiling Sensors | Badge Systems | SmartStep |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Accuracy | Room-level | Entry/exit only | Zone-level |
| Coverage | Requires line of sight | Badge holders only | 100% foot traffic |
| Privacy | Medium | Low (identified) | High (anonymous) |
| Install complexity | Medium | High | Low (under floor) |

---

### 3. Gait Analysis / PT Technology
**Competitors** (categories):
- Instrumented walkways (pressure mats)
- Motion capture systems (camera-based)
- Wearable gait sensors
- Smartphone gait apps

**Our Positioning**:
> "Clinical-grade gait analysis that captures every step, every session, without setup time or equipment limitations."

**Competitive Advantages**:
| Factor | Pressure Mats | Motion Capture | Wearables | SmartStep |
|--------|---------------|----------------|-----------|-----------|
| Always-on | No (placed for test) | No (requires setup) | Only when worn | Yes |
| Coverage area | Limited mat size | Limited capture volume | Unlimited | Entire clinic |
| Setup time | Minutes | Hours | Minutes | Zero |
| Cost | Medium | Very High | Low | Medium |
| Clinical data | Yes | Yes | Limited | Yes |

---

### 4. Building Management / IoT
**Competitors** (categories):
- Building automation systems (BAS/BMS)
- IoT sensor platforms
- Energy management systems
- Integrated workplace management systems (IWMS)

**Our Positioning**:
> "SmartStep integrates with your existing BMS to add a layer of floor-based intelligence that other sensors can't provide."

**Note**: In this category, SmartStep is often complementary rather than competitive.

## Competitive Battlecards (High-Level)

### When competing against camera-based systems:
- Lead with privacy: "No cameras means no privacy concerns"
- Resident/patient acceptance: "People don't feel watched"
- Regulatory advantage: "Simpler HIPAA compliance"
- Data richness: "We capture gait and pressure, not just presence"

### When competing against wearables:
- Lead with compliance: "100% coverage, no forgetting to wear it"
- Dignity: "No visible device that stigmatizes"
- Maintenance: "No batteries to change, no devices to lose"
- Data quality: "Continuous monitoring, not intermittent"

### When competing against traditional sensors:
- Lead with accuracy: "Millimeter-level vs. room-level"
- Data richness: "Movement patterns, not just on/off"
- Installation: "Under the floor, invisible, no maintenance"
- Analytics: "AI-driven insights, not just raw data"
