# Scanalytics Executive Summary

## Company Overview
Scanalytics transforms floors into intelligent data-collection surfaces using patented, DOE-backed smart flooring technology. The solution captures occupancy and movement data to optimize buildings across multiple verticals.

## Core Value Proposition
**"Smart Flooring" - An invisible layer of intelligence beneath any floor surface**

Turn any floor into a sensor that:
- Captures up to 100% of foot traffic anonymously
- Requires no wearables, cameras, or visible sensors
- Integrates with existing building systems via API
- Delivers actionable insights through real-time dashboards

## Key Differentiators
1. **DOE-backed, patented technology** - Government-validated innovation
2. **100% US manufactured** - Supply chain reliability
3. **Anonymous by design** - No privacy concerns (no cameras, no wearables)
4. **Works under existing flooring** - Retrofit-friendly, minimal disruption
5. **Less than 10% coverage required** - Strategic placement delivers full-building insights

## Proof Points
- Department of Energy backing
- Patented technology
- Partnerships with world's largest flooring companies
- 100% US manufacturing
- Validated against gold-standard motion capture systems (PT)
- Battery and cellular backup for continuous operation
- HIPAA-compliant (Healthcare/PT)

## Pricing Model
- **Annual subscription**: $0.25-$0.40 per square foot per year
- **One-time installation**: $0.40 per sq ft (typically 15-30% of total area)
- **Volume discounts**: Multi-building and longer-term commitments
- **Includes**: Hardware, software platform, mobile app, updates, training, support

## Strategic Assumptions
1. Privacy concerns are a primary objection - "no cameras" messaging is critical
2. Each segment requires distinct positioning and value props
3. Enterprise/B2B sales cycle with facility decision-makers
4. Integration capability is table stakes for enterprise buyers
5. ROI must be demonstrable (energy savings, labor efficiency, reduced liability)
