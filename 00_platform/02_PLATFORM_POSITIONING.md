# Platform Positioning

## Master Positioning Statement
For facility operators who need accurate, privacy-preserving occupancy and movement intelligence, Scanalytics Smart Flooring is the only floor-based sensor system that captures 100% of foot traffic anonymously while integrating with existing building systems. Unlike cameras, wearables, or motion sensors, our DOE-backed patented technology is invisible, non-invasive, and delivers actionable insights from day one.

## Positioning by Alternative

### vs. Cameras
| Dimension | Cameras | Scanalytics |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Privacy | Invasive, faces captured | Anonymous, no visual data |
| Resident/patient acceptance | Low - feel watched | High - invisible |
| Accuracy | Varies with lighting, occlusion | Consistent, 100% capture |
| Installation | Visible, requires planning | Hidden under flooring |
| Data richness | Visual only | Pressure, gait, movement patterns |

### vs. Wearables (pendants, watches)
| Dimension | Wearables | Scanalytics |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Compliance | Low - users forget/refuse | 100% - always on |
| Maintenance | Battery changes, lost devices | Zero end-user maintenance |
| Dignity | Stigmatizing for seniors | Invisible, preserves dignity |
| Coverage | Only when worn | 24/7 continuous |
| Data | Limited to wearer location | Full movement patterns |

### vs. Motion Sensors (PIR)
| Dimension | Motion Sensors | Scanalytics |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| Resolution | Room-level only | Millimeter-level accuracy |
| Data type | Presence/absence | Gait, speed, patterns |
| False positives | High (pets, HVAC) | Low - human-specific |
| Analytics | Basic counting | AI-driven insights |

## Category Creation Opportunity
**"Smart Flooring" as a new category** - not competing in existing sensor categories but defining a new one.

## Narrative Arc
1. **Floors are everywhere** - Every building has them
2. **Movement tells stories** - How people move reveals health, behavior, efficiency
3. **Current solutions have tradeoffs** - Privacy vs. data, accuracy vs. acceptance
4. **Smart Flooring solves this** - Invisible, anonymous, accurate, comprehensive
5. **From data to outcomes** - Better care, lower costs, optimized spaces
