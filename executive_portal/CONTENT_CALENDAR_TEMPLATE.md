## Monthly Publishing Cadence

### Minimum Viable Cadence (Resource-Constrained)
- **2 LinkedIn posts per week** (choose 1 segment to focus)
- **1 long-form article per month**
- **Total: ~10 pieces/month**

### Standard Cadence
- **3 LinkedIn posts per week** (rotate 2 segments)
- **2 long-form articles per month**
- **Total: ~14 pieces/month**

### Full Engine Cadence
- **5 LinkedIn posts per week** (cover all segments)
- **4 long-form articles per month**
- **Total: ~24 pieces/month**

## Approval Workflow

1. **Draft** → Content creator writes
2. **Internal Review** → Marketing lead reviews messaging
3. **Stakeholder Approval** → VP or segment owner approves claims
4. **Schedule** → Queue in LinkedIn/blog scheduler
5. **Publish** → Goes live
6. **Monitor** → Track engagement for 48 hours

**Turnaround Targets:**
- Social posts: 24-hour review cycle
- Long-form: 72-hour review cycle
