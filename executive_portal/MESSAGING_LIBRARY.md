## Objection Handling Snippets

### "Too expensive"
> "At [price range], most customers see ROI within the first year. In senior living, one prevented fall with injury pays for 2+ years. In PT, saving one hour per therapist per week covers the monthly cost."

### "Privacy/surveillance concerns"
> "SmartStep uses no cameras, no microphones, and no wearables. We capture anonymous foot traffic patterns - we never identify individuals. This is why facilities choose us over camera-based systems."

### "We have [existing solution]"
> "We complement what you have. [Pendants] only work when worn. [Bed alarms] only work in bed. [Ceiling sensors] give room-level data. SmartStep fills the gaps with continuous, precise, facility-wide coverage."

### "Integration complexity"
> "SmartStep is designed to integrate with major systems - [EMR/nurse call/BMS]. Our API handles data transfer, and our implementation team manages the technical integration."

### "Installation disruption"
> "SmartStep installs beneath existing flooring with minimal disruption. Most installations complete in a day. We work off-hours when needed. No flooring replacement required."

### "Staff won't use it"
> "SmartStep works with your existing workflows. Alerts go to systems staff already use. There's no new interface to learn - we add intelligence, not complexity."

### "Not ready / need to think about it"
> "I understand. Would a pilot in one [unit/zone/building] help you evaluate? We can start small and expand based on results."

## Do Not Say (Risky Claims)

| Avoid This | Why | Say This Instead |
|------------|-----|------------------|
| "Saves 35% on energy" | Unvalidated claim | "Enables occupancy-based energy control" |
| "Clinically validated" | No published study cited | "Millimeter-level accuracy" |
| "Prevents falls" | Cannot guarantee prevention | "Detects falls instantly" |
| "Thousands of customers" | Unverified count | "Trusted by facilities across [markets]" |
| "94% peace of mind" | No survey methodology | Remove or note "in customer surveys" |
| "Best fall detection" | Comparative claim | "Comprehensive fall detection" |
| Specific customer names | Without permission | "A leading [segment] operator" |
| FDA cleared | Not applicable | Remove entirely |
| "Guarantees outcomes" | Cannot guarantee | "Enables better outcomes" |
