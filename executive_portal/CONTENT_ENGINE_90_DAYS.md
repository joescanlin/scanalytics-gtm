## How to Use This Document

Each week has:
- **Theme**: The unifying concept
- **5 Post Prompts per Segment**: LinkedIn-style posts (each tagged with persona and objective)
- **1 Longer-Form Outline per Segment**: Blog post or article (800-1200 words)

**Persona Tags:**
- [BUYER] = Decision maker (Clinic Owner, ED, Facility Manager, Administrator, Performance Director)
- [INFLUENCER] = Daily user or recommender (PT, DON, Ops Manager, Safety Officer, Athletic Trainer)

**Objective Tags:**
- [AWARENESS] = Problem/solution awareness
- [TRUST] = Credibility and proof building
- [CONVERSION] = Direct action/demo request
