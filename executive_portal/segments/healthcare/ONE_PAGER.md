## ICP Summary

### Firmographics
- **Facility size:** 100+ beds
- **Facility type:** Acute care hospitals, rehab facilities, long-term acute care
- **Square footage:** 100,000-1,000,000+ sq ft
- **Setting:** Single hospital or health system

### Buyer Roles
| Role | Decision Power | Cares About |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Hospital Administrator | Final budget authority | Liability, quality scores, costs |
| CNO (Chief Nursing Officer) | Strong influence | Patient safety, staff efficiency |
| VP of Quality / Patient Safety | Requirements definition | Metrics, compliance, prevention |
| CFO | Budget approval | ROI, liability reduction |

### Demand Triggers
- Serious fall with injury or death
- Quality score drop (CMS measures)
- Litigation or settlement
- Accreditation preparation
- New construction or renovation

### Exclusions (Don't Pursue)
- Very small facilities (<50 beds)
- No patient safety budget
- Recent camera system investment
- No fall prevention initiative

## Problems and Desired Outcomes

### Problems They Feel
1. "Falls happen even with bed alarms and call buttons"
2. "Our fall rate affects CMS reimbursement"
3. "Liability from fall injuries is a major cost"
4. "We can't monitor every patient room 24/7"
5. "Documentation of fall prevention is burdensome"

### Outcomes They Want
1. Detect falls instantly, anywhere in facility
2. Improve CMS quality scores
3. Reduce fall-related liability and litigation
4. Extend monitoring without adding cameras
5. Automated documentation for compliance

## 5 Approved Message Bullets (Copy/Paste)

1. **"Hospital-wide fall detection, HIPAA-compliant."** SmartStep detects falls the moment they happen and alerts staff with precise location - all without collecting protected health information.

2. **"Beyond the bed alarm."** Bed alarms and call buttons have gaps. SmartStep monitors the entire patient journey - bathroom, hallway, room - 24/7.

3. **"Improve quality scores with data."** Track fall rates, response times, and mobility patterns to support quality improvement and CMS reporting.

4. **"Integrates with your EMR."** Fall events and mobility data flow directly into Epic, Cerner, and other EMR systems for seamless documentation.

5. **"Battery and cellular backup."** System stays on during power outages and network failures. Your patients are always protected.

## Top Objections and Best Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is it HIPAA compliant?" | "Yes. We collect no PHI - floor sensors detect movement patterns, not identities. All data stored in HIPAA-compliant infrastructure." |
| "Installation disruption" | "We work off-hours and phase by unit. Sensors go beneath existing flooring with minimal disruption." |
| "EMR integration?" | "We integrate with Epic, Cerner, and other major systems via API." |
| "We have bed alarms" | "Bed alarms only work in bed. We monitor the whole patient journey - bathroom, hallway, everywhere." |
| "Budget constraints" | "One prevented fall with injury can cost $40,000+. SmartStep pays for itself in reduced incidents." |
