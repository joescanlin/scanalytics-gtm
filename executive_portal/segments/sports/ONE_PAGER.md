## ICP Summary

### Firmographics
- **Organization type:** Professional sports teams, elite college programs, national federations
- **Facility:** Dedicated training facility or performance center
- **Budget:** Performance technology budget available
- **Sports:** Any sport with gait/movement relevance

### Buyer Roles
| Role | Decision Power | Cares About |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Director of Performance / Sports Science | Primary decision | Data quality, integration, edge |
| Head Coach | Final authority | Competitive advantage, adoption |
| Athletic Trainer / Team Physician | Strong influence | Injury prevention, RTH |
| GM / Director of Athletics | Budget authority | ROI, risk reduction |

### Demand Triggers
- Key player injury (especially preventable)
- New training facility build-out
- Competitive pressure (rival has better tech)
- New performance director hire
- Research partnership opportunity

### Exclusions (Don't Pursue)
- Recreational or youth programs
- No performance staff
- No technology budget
- Single-sport focus that doesn't involve running/movement

## Problems and Desired Outcomes

### Problems They Feel
1. "We don't see injury risks until it's too late"
2. "Force plate testing is periodic - we miss what happens between tests"
3. "Return-to-play decisions are based on feel, not data"
4. "Our competitors have better performance technology"
5. "Wearables give us macro data, not movement quality"

### Outcomes They Want
1. Continuous movement monitoring during actual training
2. Early detection of asymmetries and injury risk
3. Objective return-to-play criteria
4. Competitive edge through better data
5. Movement quality data to complement load data

## 5 Approved Message Bullets (Copy/Paste)

1. **"Continuous movement capture during actual training."** Unlike periodic force plate testing, SmartStep captures every step, jump, and cut during real training sessions.

2. **"See injury risks before they become injuries."** AI analysis detects movement asymmetries and pattern changes that precede soft tissue injuries.

3. **"Objective return-to-play data."** Compare post-injury movement patterns to pre-injury baselines for data-driven RTH decisions.

4. **"No wearables, no setup."** The floor captures data automatically - athletes train naturally while you get comprehensive movement analytics.

5. **"Integrates with your performance platform."** API connection to existing sports analytics systems means movement data joins your load, wellness, and GPS data.
