## ICP Summary

### Firmographics
- **Clinic size:** 5,000-25,000 sq ft
- **Patient volume:** 50+ patients/week
- **Staff:** 3+ treating therapists
- **Revenue:** $500K+ annually
- **Setting:** Outpatient, private practice, or hospital-affiliated

### Buyer Roles
| Role | Decision Power | Cares About |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Clinic Owner / Practice Manager | Final decision, budget | ROI, differentiation, efficiency |
| Physical Therapist | Strong influence, daily user | Clinical utility, time savings |
| Director of Rehab (hospital) | Budget for department | Outcomes, quality metrics |

### Demand Triggers
- Frustration with documentation time
- Reimbursement pressure from payers
- Competitor has better technology
- Looking for objective outcome data
- Renovation or new clinic opening

### Exclusions (Don't Pursue)
- Solo practitioners (too small)
- Home health / mobile PT only
- No interest in objective measures
- Extremely price-sensitive
