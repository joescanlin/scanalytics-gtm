## ICP Summary

### Firmographics
- **Community size:** 50-200+ units/beds
- **Facility type:** Independent Living, Assisted Living, Memory Care, CCRC
- **Square footage:** 25,000-150,000+ sq ft
- **Staff:** 20+ caregiving staff
- **Ownership:** Private operators, regional chains, national portfolios

### Buyer Roles
| Role | Decision Power | Cares About |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Executive Director | Final decision, budget | Liability, quality, differentiation |
| Director of Nursing | Strong influence | Resident safety, staff efficiency |
| VP of Operations (portfolio) | Multi-site decisions | Scalability, standardization, ROI |
| Regional Director | Pilot authority | Proof of concept, expansion |

### Demand Triggers
- Recent fall incident (especially with injury)
- Staffing crisis / can't fill overnight shifts
- Family complaint about monitoring
- Renovation or new construction
- Competitor technology announcement

### Exclusions (Don't Pursue)
- Very small communities (<30 units)
- No technology budget
- Recent investment in camera system
- No concern about fall rates

## Problems and Desired Outcomes

### Problems They Feel
1. "We can't watch everyone - falls happen when no one's looking"
2. "Residents won't wear their pendants"
3. "Families ask if we're monitoring and we can't say yes without cameras"
4. "Staffing is impossible - we need technology to extend our team"
5. "One fall with injury costs us $40,000+"

### Outcomes They Want
1. Detect every fall, day or night
2. 100% coverage without compliance issues
3. Give families peace of mind without invasive cameras
4. Do more with fewer staff
5. Reduce fall-related liability and costs

5. **"Battery and cellular backup."** System stays on during power outages and network failures. Your residents are always protected.

## 30-Day Campaign Idea

### Theme: "The Fall-Free Facility"

**Week 1: Problem Awareness**
- Stats on fall costs and frequency
- Poll: "What's your biggest fall prevention challenge?"
- Story: "What happens after a fall" (staff, family, liability)

**Week 2: Solution Introduction**
- "What if floors could detect falls?"
- Privacy angle: "Monitoring without cameras"
- Staff efficiency angle: "Fewer night rounds, same safety"

**Week 3: Proof and Credibility**
- Technology explainer
- DOE-backed credibility
- Installation simplicity story

**Week 4: Conversion Push**
- Free facility assessment offer
- Pilot program invitation
- Urgency: "Before your next fall incident"

**Success Metric:** 5 facility demos scheduled
