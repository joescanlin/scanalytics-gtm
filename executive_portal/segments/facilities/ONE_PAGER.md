## ICP Summary

### Firmographics
- **Building size:** 50,000-500,000+ sq ft
- **Building type:** Corporate offices, commercial, education, hospitality
- **Ownership:** Owner-occupied, property management, corporate RE
- **Team:** Dedicated facilities/operations team

### Buyer Roles
| Role | Decision Power | Cares About |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Facility Manager / Director | Primary decision, budget | Cost reduction, efficiency, data |
| VP of Real Estate | Portfolio decisions | Space utilization, cost per sq ft |
| Director of Operations | Day-to-day ops | Staff efficiency, service quality |
| Sustainability Lead | ESG influence | Energy reduction, reporting |

### Demand Triggers
- Return-to-office space planning
- Cleaning cost review / budget pressure
- Energy audit or ESG initiative
- New building management system
- Post-COVID occupancy uncertainty

### Exclusions (Don't Pursue)
- Very small buildings (<10,000 sq ft)
- No facilities budget
- Strong preference for camera-based systems
- No interest in data-driven operations

## Problems and Desired Outcomes

### Problems They Feel
1. "We clean empty offices on a schedule, not based on need"
2. "I have no idea how our space is actually being used"
3. "Leadership wants data on occupancy and I can't give it"
4. "Energy costs are up and we're conditioning empty spaces"
5. "We need to justify real estate decisions with data"

### Outcomes They Want
1. Clean based on traffic, not schedule (save 15-25% on cleaning)
2. Accurate space utilization data for planning
3. Occupancy dashboards for leadership
4. Reduce energy waste with occupancy-based controls
5. Data to optimize real estate portfolio

## 5 Approved Message Bullets (Copy/Paste)

1. **"Know how your space is actually used."** SmartStep captures real traffic patterns - not badge swipes or surveys. See which areas are busy, which are empty, and when.

2. **"Clean smarter, not more."** Traffic-based cleaning schedules mean high-traffic areas get attention while empty spaces get skipped. Typical savings: 15-25% on cleaning labor.

3. **"Less than 10% coverage delivers full-building insights."** Strategic sensor placement at entrances, corridors, and key zones gives you comprehensive data without instrumenting every room.

4. **"No cameras, no privacy concerns."** Anonymous foot traffic data gives you what you need without employee surveillance concerns.

5. **"Integrates with your BMS."** API connection to building management systems enables occupancy-based HVAC, lighting, and more.
