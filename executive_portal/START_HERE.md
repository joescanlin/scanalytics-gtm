## What Scanalytics Is (60 Seconds)

**Scanalytics makes floors smart.** We embed sensors beneath any flooring surface to capture movement and occupancy data - no cameras, no wearables. This invisible layer of intelligence lets buildings understand how people move through them.

**One platform, five markets:** Physical therapy clinics use it for gait analysis. Senior living facilities use it for fall detection. Hospitals use it for patient safety. Corporate buildings use it for space optimization. Sports teams use it for performance analytics.

**Why it wins:** 100% anonymous (no privacy concerns), always-on (no compliance issues), DOE-backed technology, 100% US-manufactured.
