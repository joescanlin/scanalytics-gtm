## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Claims can't be validated | Mark as TODO, use softer language |
| No testimonials available | Use anonymized references, remove section |
| Website fixes delayed | Focus content on segments with working sites |
| Low initial engagement | Adjust targeting, test different formats |
| No demo requests | Review CTA placement, offer lower-friction option |
