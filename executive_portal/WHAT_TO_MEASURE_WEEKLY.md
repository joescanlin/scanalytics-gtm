## Metric Definitions

### 1. Website Visits
- **Source:** Google Analytics or website analytics
- **What counts:** All sessions to any Scanalytics domain
- **Watch for:** Segment breakdown (which sites getting traffic)

### 2. Demo Requests
- **Source:** Form submissions
- **What counts:** "Schedule Demo" or "Request Demo" completions
- **Watch for:** Source attribution (organic vs. paid vs. direct)

### 3. LinkedIn Impressions
- **Source:** LinkedIn Analytics
- **What counts:** Total views of all company posts
- **Watch for:** Per-post performance to identify winners

### 4. LinkedIn Engagement Rate
- **Source:** LinkedIn Analytics
- **Formula:** (Reactions + Comments + Shares + Clicks) / Impressions
- **Watch for:** Which segments and topics drive engagement

### 5. Email Open Rate
- **Source:** Email platform (HubSpot, Mailchimp, etc.)
- **What counts:** Unique opens / Delivered emails
- **Watch for:** Subject line performance, send time impact

### 6. Email Click Rate
- **Source:** Email platform
- **What counts:** Unique clicks / Delivered emails
- **Watch for:** CTA effectiveness, content relevance

### 7. Cost Per Lead
- **Source:** Ad platforms + CRM
- **Formula:** Total ad spend / Total leads generated
- **Watch for:** Segment differences, channel efficiency

### 8. Demos Completed
- **Source:** Sales calendar / CRM
- **What counts:** Demos that actually happened (not no-shows)
- **Watch for:** Show rate (completed / scheduled)

### 9. Pipeline Value Added
- **Source:** CRM
- **What counts:** New opportunity value created this week
- **Watch for:** Average deal size by segment

### 10. Content Pieces Published
- **Source:** Your calendar
- **What counts:** Posts, articles, and assets that went live
- **Watch for:** Consistency vs. plan

## Segment-Specific Additions

For each active segment, also track:

### Physical Therapy
- PT-specific site visits
- "AI documentation" search impressions (if running SEO)

### Senior Living
- Fall-related keyword rankings
- Senior living site demo requests specifically

### Facilities
- "Space utilization" search impressions
- Facilities site engagement

### Healthcare
- Hospital-specific demo requests
- Healthcare compliance content engagement

### Sports
- Sports performance site visits
- Conference/event leads (when applicable)

## Red Flags to Watch

| If You See This... | It Might Mean... |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Traffic up, demos flat | Wrong audience or weak CTA |
| High impressions, low engagement | Content not resonating |
| High open rate, low click rate | Email content doesn't match subject |
| Demos up, pipeline flat | Lead quality issue |
| Content velocity dropping | Resource/process problem |

---

## Monthly Roll-Up

At month end, aggregate weekly data into:

1. **Total demo requests** (vs. target)
2. **Total pipeline created** (vs. target)
3. **Content published vs. planned** (% completion)
4. **Top 3 performing posts** (by engagement)
5. **Top 3 traffic sources** (by demo conversion)
6. **Cost per lead trend** (improving or not)
