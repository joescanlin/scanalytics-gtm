## Segment Priority Recommendation

### If starting from scratch, prioritize by:

**Fastest to close:** Physical Therapy (shortest cycle, smallest deal, simpler decision)

**Highest revenue per deal:** Healthcare (largest deals, but longest cycle)

**Best proof story potential:** Senior Living (clearest ROI narrative around falls)

**Biggest expansion opportunity:** Facilities (every building is a prospect, ESG tailwinds)

**Most differentiated positioning:** Sports (least crowded, premium positioning possible)

## Cross-Segment Messaging Consistency

All segments share these core messages:
- "No cameras, no wearables" (privacy/dignity)
- "Works beneath existing flooring" (retrofit-friendly)
- "DOE-backed, patented technology" (credibility)
- "100% US manufactured" (supply chain)
- "Battery + cellular backup" (reliability)

Segment-specific variations exist for:
- Compliance language (HIPAA for PT/Healthcare)
- Outcome framing (gait analysis vs. fall detection vs. occupancy)
- Integration targets (EMR vs. nurse call vs. BMS)
